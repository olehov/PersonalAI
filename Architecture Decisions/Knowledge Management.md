
# Knowledge Management

## Types of Knowledge

### Projects

Long-term project documentation.

Examples:

- ServerX
- Inception
- PersonalAI

---

### Algorithms

Reusable algorithmic knowledge.

Examples:

- Heap
- Binary Search
- Dynamic Programming

---

### Bugs

Documented failures and fixes.

---

### Optimizations

Performance improvements.

---

### Lessons Learned

General conclusions extracted from experience.

---

## Knowledge Lifecycle

New Information
↓
Inbox
↓
Classification
↓
Project / Algorithm / Bug / Lesson
↓
Knowledge Base

---

## Goals

Reduce duplication.

Prefer updating existing knowledge over creating new notes.

Prefer structured knowledge over raw logs.
