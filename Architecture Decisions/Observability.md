# Observability

## Basics
Observability is the ability to understand the internal state of a system by examining its outputs and runtime behavior.

## Core Signals

- logs
- metrics
- traces

## Why It Matters
Observability helps engineers detect failures, explain performance problems, and debug distributed systems under real load.

## Practical Examples

- inspecting service logs from [[Systemd]]
- tracing request flow through [[HTTP]] services
- measuring latency and error rates behind [[Load Balancing]]
- correlating process failures with [[Processes and Signals]]

## Common Questions

- what failed?
- where did latency increase?
- is the problem local or distributed?
